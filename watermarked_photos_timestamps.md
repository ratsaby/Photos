# Norway Photographs — OpenTimestamps Record

This collection contains watermarked photographs taken by Joel Ratsaby during trips to Norway.

Each photograph is accompanied by an OpenTimestamps proof file (`.ots`) and a SHA-256 hash.

The timestamps anchor the photograph hashes in the Bitcoin blockchain, providing cryptographic proof that the corresponding files existed at or before the recorded time.

The photographs published here are watermarked copies. The original, unwatermarked photographs and their corresponding OpenTimestamps proofs are retained privately by Joel Ratsaby.
